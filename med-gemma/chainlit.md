# 💊 MedGuard — Prescription & Interaction Inspector

Paste a prescription photo or type your medicines (one per line).
I transcribe, resolve Indian brands to active ingredients, and check them
against reference data — then explain the findings in plain language.

**The AI explains; it never decides.** Every safety claim comes from a
deterministic rules engine (DDInter pairs, CDSCO banned-FDC list, Schedule H1)
and carries its source. Anything uncertain is labeled `UNVERIFIED` or left
`unresolved` for you to confirm — never guessed.

⚠️ *Informational safety aid, not a diagnosis. Always confirm with a
pharmacist or clinician. Do not start, stop, or change any dose on the basis
of this review.*
